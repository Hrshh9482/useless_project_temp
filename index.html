🧠 How Kuttan Works

A surprisingly sophisticated cat with zero tolerance for productivity

👁️
STEP 1

Monitors Windows

Kuttan watches your active windows and browser tabs in real-time using Win32 API and UI Automation.

😾
STEP 2

Gets Irritated

Opens VS Code? LeetCode? ChatGPT? PDF? Kuttan's eyes turn red and he starts fuming with anger.

🚶
STEP 3

Walks to Target

Using UI Automation, Kuttan locates the exact tab position on screen and walks over with animated legs.

💥
STEP 4

Paw Smash!

Kuttan raises his paw and SMASHES — sending Ctrl+W for browser tabs or WM_CLOSE for desktop apps.

😸
BONUS

Loves Distractions

Open Instagram or Netflix? Kuttan sits happily, purrs, and encourages you to keep scrolling.

😴
IDLE

Sleeps & Giggles

No activity for 2 seconds? Kuttan curls up and sleeps. Click him for giggles, hearts, and a meow!
